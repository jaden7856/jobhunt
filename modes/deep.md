# Mode: deep — company research

A short research note on one company and team, used for the cover letter ("why this company"), interview reverse questions, and offer decisions. Read `modes/_shared.md` first.

## What to collect

| Item | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Product and users, business model, recent launches | company site, app store, press releases |
| The team behind the posting: what it owns, stack, scale | the posting, tech blog, conference talks, GitHub |
| Engineering culture: deploy practice, on-call, code review, tests | tech blog, talks, posting wording |
| Company state: funding, revenue trend, headcount, layoffs | news, Wanted company info, public filings |
| Hiring process and interview reports | posting, public interview reviews (cite, don't copy) |
| Red flags | overtime culture, bonus-heavy pay, repeated reposting, turnover signals (`modes/interview.md` "redflag") |

Use web search or a browser when the host has one; otherwise ask the user for links and work from what they give. Every claim gets a source link and the date it was read; anything unverified is marked "확인 못 함".

## Output

`data/job_postings/<same name as the posting>.deep.md` (Korean): 한 줄 요약 · 제품과 사용자 · 이 팀이 하는 일 · 기술과 규모 · 개발 문화 · 회사 상태 · 경고 신호 · 이 회사여야 하는 이유(내 경험과 맞닿는 점 2~3개) · 역질문 후보 3개 · 출처.
